Chapter is a reading tracker. This explains what it stores, why, and how to remove it.

WHAT CHAPTER STORES

Your account. When you sign in with Google or Apple: your name, email address and profile photo, as provided by that service. Apple may give Chapter a private relay address instead of your email.

Your reading. Books in your library, shelves, reading progress, reading sessions, notes, quotes, ratings, reviews, goals and audiobook editions you add. This is stored on your device and synced to our servers so it can reach your other devices.

Your settings. Theme and your choices about analytics and crash reports.

Files you import. Goodreads, StoryGraph and Kindle files are read on your device. The file itself is not uploaded. What you choose to import becomes part of your reading data, and each book's title, author and ISBN is looked up through our servers.

Camera. Used only to read book barcodes, and only when you open the scanner. Images are not stored or sent anywhere.

WHAT CHAPTER SENDS TO OTHERS

Book lookups. Searches and ISBNs go to our servers, which ask book data services (Open Library, and Hardcover and Google Books where enabled). Your account details aren't included in these requests. Our servers cache book information, which is public data about books, not about you.

Spotify. Only if you choose to link your Spotify account: Chapter keeps your Spotify login tokens and display name on our servers, shows your playlists and what's playing, and can start or pause music on your devices. We don't store what you listen to or build statistics from it. Unlinking in Profile deletes the tokens and name straight away, and so does deleting your account.

Google Firebase. Chapter's sign-in, database and server functions run on Firebase, provided by Google, which processes your data on our behalf.

Analytics. Off unless you turn it on, during setup or in Profile. Analytics events never include book titles, notes or searches.

Crash reports. On by default, and you can turn them off during setup or in Profile. A crash report contains the type of error, where in the code it happened and basic device details, not the content of your library or notes.

Links. Spotify and Audible links open in those services, under their own privacy policies. Chapter doesn't connect to your accounts there and can't see what you do.

Chapter doesn't sell your data, show ads or share your reading with other people. There are no public profiles.

HOW LONG IT'S KEPT

Your data is kept until you delete it or delete your account.

YOUR CHOICES

Export. Profile, Export my data gives you your data as JSON and CSV files, without needing a connection. The account email is not included in the export.

Delete your account. Profile, Delete account permanently removes your account and reading data from our servers and from that device. Other devices signed in to the account stop syncing, and remove their copy the next time they open Chapter with a connection. A device that was online at that moment may be able to send changes for up to an hour afterwards; those are removed automatically soon after.

You can also ask us about your data, or to correct or delete it, at the contact address above.

SECURITY

Data is encrypted in transit. Server rules allow each account to reach only its own data. The copy on your device is protected by your device's security and isn't included in Android's cloud backups.

CHILDREN

Chapter isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
