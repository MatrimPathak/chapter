**App name:** Chapter — Reading Tracker (24)

**Short description (Play, 80 max):**
Track your books, time your reading and keep your notes. Private by design. (74)

**Subtitle (App Store, 30 max):**
Your reading life, in one place (31 — one over; "Your reading, in one place" is 26)

**Full description:**

Chapter is a calm place for your reading life. Pick a book, read, record the session, and pick up where you left off.

TRACK EVERY BOOK
Search or scan a barcode to add a book. Keep shelves for what you're reading, want to read, have finished or set aside.

ONE BOOK, ANY FORMAT
Read in print, listen on the commute, and keep one place in the book. Progress carries between editions, and Chapter tells you when a position is approximate.

TIME YOUR READING
A focus timer that survives closing the app and restarts. Turn on Do Not Disturb for the session if you like.

GOALS AND HONEST INSIGHTS
Set book, page or time goals. See your reading calendar and habits. Chapter won't show a statistic until it has enough data for it to mean something.

NOTES THAT STAY YOURS
Keep notes and quotes with page numbers. Rate and review for yourself. No public profiles, no ads, no feed.

BRING YOUR HISTORY
Import from Goodreads and The StoryGraph, and bring Kindle highlights from My Clippings.txt.

WORKS OFFLINE
Everything you do is saved on your device first and syncs when you're back online.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CALL
Export everything at any time. Delete your account for good from inside the app.

A note on connected services: Audible and Kindle don't offer public APIs, so Chapter can't read your progress from them automatically. You log it yourself in a few taps. Spotify links open Spotify; Chapter doesn't connect to your account.
